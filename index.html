Jenna for Treasurer

“The hand of the diligent makes rich.” — Proverbs 10:4

I love our Liahona community, and I would be honored to serve and support the entire student body as your treasurer!

What I hope to accomplish this year

01. Opportunities for the people

I'd love to introduce after-school and weekend clubs where students can explore shared interests and connect with each other! CLubs would offer a flexible, affordable way to try new things without the financial or scheduling commitments that can come with electives.

Whether it's a book club, art club, or something entirely new, I'd love to see more chances for students to pursue what they enjoy!

02. A greater share for everyone

Service is a huge part of Liahona, and I want to expand the opportunities we have to give back! My goal is to organize more service projects throughout the year, with scheduling and costs in mind so everyone has a chance to participate.

By making thoughtful use of shared supplies and resources andoppering a variety of opportunities, we can make service something even more students can enjoy and contribute to!

03. Regognition and appreciation for all

I'd love to see our tradition of celebrating student achievements continue to grow! After last year's sports awards, I hope to bring them back and make them an even bigger event, as well as working toward introducing academic awards in the future.

I'd also love to find ways to show further appreciation for our teachers! From making sure everyone knows how to participate in Teacher Appreciation Week to creating additional opportunities to recognize our teachers throughout the year, there's always a reason for us to show our gratitude.
